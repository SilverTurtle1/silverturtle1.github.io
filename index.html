About

I've spent my career turning data into decisions. From 2005 to 2019 I ran services and operations at four SaaS companies in banking, payments, and insurance — ACI Worldwide, Alkami, Decideware, and One Inc — and analysis was always at the center of the job. At One Inc, I designed the Tableau dashboard suite that put Sales, Delivery, Support, and Success on the same numbers. At Decideware, I helped develop and sell a Tableau advisory service that let our Fortune 500 clients analyze their own advertising spend; it grew revenue 98% in a year. At Alkami, a VC-backed startup, I led capacity planning, building the labor models that kept project delivery and revenue recognition on track with board plans while the company doubled its headcount in six months.

In 2019 I left to trade full-time, where every decision gets tested against data and real money. I built my own market-data infrastructure and research process; my ES futures data pipeline shows the standard I hold analysis to. I'm now looking for senior individual-contributor roles where judgment about data drives the work, not producing reports for someone else to interpret.

FINRA Off-Exchange Short Volume Dashboard

A live web app that turns FINRA's daily off-exchange short-volume data, about 12,000 symbols a day, into an interactive treemap, with a daily panel that flags volume surges and short-% shifts against each stock's own 20-day average.
